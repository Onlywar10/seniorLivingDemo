# Brand artwork

- **Wrenhaven mark** (`dots.png`, `dots.svg`, and the bird in `wrenhaven-white.png`): recoloured from
  [Wren 2 (PSF).png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wren_2_(PSF).png), a line drawing from the
  Pearson Scott Foresman donation to Wikimedia Commons, released into the **public domain**.
- **Wordmark lettering** in `wrenhaven-white.png`: Libre Baskerville, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **Values icons** (`values/*.svg`): drawn for this project.

Wrenhaven Senior Living, its communities, people and places are fictional.
